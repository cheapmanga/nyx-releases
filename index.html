It lives inside Steam

What Nyx touches on your PC

The plugin ships as plain Lua and JavaScript and the app isn't obfuscated, so anyone can check this list.

What it changes

  • Puts the unlock engine in your Steam folder so added games can install.
  • Writes game .lua and .manifest files into Steam's config and depot cache.
  • Keeps its own settings in %LocalAppData%\Nyx, never your Steam credentials.
  • Nothing else: no account login, no tracking of what you play.

How to undo it

  • A game: open its store page, then Nyx icon ▸ Remove via Nyx.
  • Nyx itself: use the off switch in Steam ▸ Nyx, or delete the engine files and the Nyx plugin folder.
  • Removing Nyx leaves your installed games where they are.

Questions people ask

Do I need to own the game?
No. Nyx writes the manifest and depot files Steam needs, then Steam downloads the game from its own servers like any other title.
Will my games stay up to date?
Yes. Nyx tracks the build Steam is shipping right now and moves your added games to it, so they install and update to the current version instead of an old snapshot.
What about online games and Denuvo?
Nyx installs the game. A protected or online-only title can still need a separate fix, which Nyx doesn't bundle. Single-player games generally just work once installed. Read about fixes and online play.
Is it safe for my account?
Nyx only writes local manifest and .lua files and deploys its unlock engine. It never logs into your account, never asks for your password and never modifies game files. As with any tool of this kind, keep added multiplayer games off VAC-secured servers.
Why does my antivirus flag the download?
The build isn't code-signed (certificates are expensive) and it writes into Steam's folders, which heuristic scanners treat as suspicious. The plugin is plain Lua and JavaScript and the app is unobfuscated, so anyone can read what it does.
Standalone or Millennium plugin?
Take the standalone app if you're not sure: it sets everything up on its own. Pick the Millennium plugin only if you already run Millennium.
How do I remove a game, or Nyx itself?
For a game: its store page, then Nyx icon ▸ Remove via Nyx. For Nyx: the off switch in Steam ▸ Nyx, or delete the engine files and the Nyx plugin folder. Your installed games stay put.
